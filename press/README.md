## One line

A free 3D action RPG in your browser: endless dungeons below, a home to build above.

## One paragraph

Hearthdeep is a village on the lip of a hole with no bottom. You delve through endless procedurally generated floors, fight a hundred kinds of foes and a boss every fifth depth, and carry the loot back up to craft at your bench and grow a leaky shack into a keep. It has four classes with talent trees, sets, gems and legendary powers, timed rifts you can race friends through with a single link, and three camera views from overhead to first person. It runs in a browser tab with nothing to download, on a phone as well as a desktop, and every texture, model, sound and piece of music in it is generated in code.

## What sets it apart

- A full action RPG that loads in a browser tab in a few seconds; the whole game is under half a megabyte to download, compressed.
- No art or audio files: textures, creatures, sound effects and the score are all procedural.
- Overhead, third-person and first-person views of the same game, switchable mid-fight.
- Challenge links: every rift has a seed code, and a link drops a friend into the same floors.
- Accessibility built in: colour-blind palettes, interface scaling, reduce-motion, three difficulties, key rebinding, screen-reader roles.
- Open source, with a headless autoplay bot that plays every class through the game as a test.

## By the numbers

10 biomes of 5 depths each, then endless · 4 classes · 36 talents · 100 enemy types · 10 champion affixes · a boss for every biome and 5 Rift Guardians · 16 legendary powers · 28 achievements · 3 camera views · 3 save slots
